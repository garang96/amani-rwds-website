# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JavaScript, deployed to GitHub Pages by the existing Actions workflow. No build step, no logins, no database. Fonts are self-hosted so the site works on slow connections and never calls out to a font service.

## Users

Two audiences, served equally (confirmed by Alier, Nov 2026):

1. **People who need AMANI:** refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities in Nakuru County, Kenya, and their parents and caregivers. Many read English as a second or third language, many use phones on slow or expensive data, and some have low vision, are Deaf or hard of hearing, or use screen readers.
2. **People who can back AMANI:** partner organisations (UNHCR and other UN agencies, NGOs, organisations of persons with disabilities, government offices), donors, volunteers and professionals. They arrive checking whether AMANI is real, well governed and worth funding.

## Product Purpose

The website is AMANI-RWDs' public front door. It must let a refugee with a disability, or their caregiver, understand in seconds that AMANI exists for them and how to reach it; and let a partner or donor confirm that AMANI is a properly constituted, accountable, refugee-led organisation and start a conversation.

## Positioning

AMANI-RWDs is led by refugees with disabilities themselves, in Nakuru, working where displacement and disability meet. Most services are built for refugees, or for persons with disabilities; AMANI is for people who are both, and it is run by them.

## Operating Context

- Registered office in Nakuru County, Kenya. Branch offices may open elsewhere in Kenya with General Assembly approval.
- Governed by a General Assembly (quarterly: April, August, December) and an elected Executive Committee (Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary, Vice Secretary, Treasurer), three-year terms.
- Membership categories: Founder, Ordinary, Associate, Honorary. Registration fee Kshs 1,000, non-refundable.
- Bank: Equity Bank, Nakuru Branch (Gate House). Account numbers are never published on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Informational only: no accounts, portals or online payments.
- Fourteen program areas from the Constitution (Article 7), plus vocational training and sports, arts and culture.
- Undecided / still to come from the organisation: email, phone, physical and postal address, social accounts, real photographs, numbers of people served, any named partners or funders.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AMANI Refugees with Disabilities, short form AMANI-RWDs. "Amani" is Swahili for peace.
- Logo: the circular emblem (dove, wheelchair user, five figures reaching up, two protecting hands, rising sun). Used as supplied, never redrawn or recoloured.
- Motto: "Empowering Abilities, Inspiring Inclusion, Transforming Lives."

## Evidence on Hand

- The organisation's Constitution (source of all governance, values, objectives and membership facts).
- The logo (`assets/images/logo.png`).
- Founder member names (11), already public on the previous site.
- No photographs, statistics, testimonials or partner logos. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The person with a disability reads first: every page works with a screen reader, at 200% zoom, on a cheap phone on 3G.
2. Plain words over development-sector jargon; one idea per sentence.
3. Show governance, don't claim trust: the Constitution's own facts do the persuading.
4. Two doors, always visible: get support, or give support.
5. Nothing invented. Missing facts stay missing until AMANI supplies them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor, AAA contrast for body text. Respects reduced motion, works without JavaScript, readable with large text. The organisation offers sign language interpretation, Braille, large print and accessible venues where practicable; the site says so.
