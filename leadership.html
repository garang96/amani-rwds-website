Leadership & Founders

AMANI-RWDs is governed by a General Assembly — the supreme decision-making organ — supported by an Executive Committee of elected office bearers who serve three-year terms, renewable once.

Governance Structure

Executive Committee Office Bearers

Elected democratically by two-thirds majority at the AGM or a Special General Meeting, by secret ballot unless the General Assembly agrees otherwise.

Chairperson

The chief executive leader of the Organization.

  • Provides overall leadership and strategic direction
  • Chairs all General Assembly and Executive Committee meetings
  • Represents the Organization before government, donors, partners, courts and stakeholders
  • Authorized bank signatory and casts the deciding vote in the event of a tie

Vice Chairperson

Assists the Chairperson in all official duties.

  • Performs the functions of the Chairperson during absence or incapacity
  • Coordinates committee activities and monitors implementation of resolutions
  • Promotes teamwork and collaboration among members

Secretary

The chief administrative officer of the Organization.

  • Convenes meetings, prepares notices, agendas and keeps minutes
  • Maintains the membership register and keeps custody of the Constitution, common seal and official records
  • Prepares annual reports and ensures regulatory compliance
  • Authorized bank signatory

Vice Secretary

Assists the Secretary in administrative duties.

  • Acts as Secretary in their absence
  • Supports documentation, record management and communication among members

Treasurer

Keeper of the Organization's financial integrity.

  • Keeps accurate financial records and prepares annual and quarterly financial reports
  • Monitors income and expenditure, and ensures proper banking of funds
  • Presents financial statements to members and works with auditors
  • Authorized bank signatory
How elections work. Elected officials hold office for 3 years, up to a maximum of 3 terms, and are eligible for one additional consecutive term. Financial transactions require any two authorized signatories, with the Chairperson's signature mandatory.
