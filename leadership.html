Leadership and governance

The members are in charge. The General Assembly makes the big decisions, and an elected Executive Committee runs AMANI between meetings.

The Executive Committee

Five office bearers, elected by the members by secret ballot for three-year terms.

Chairperson

Leads the organisation.

  • Sets overall direction
  • Chairs General Assembly and Executive Committee meetings
  • Represents AMANI to government, donors and partners
  • Bank signatory; casts the deciding vote in a tie

Vice Chairperson

Supports the Chairperson.

  • Acts as Chairperson when needed
  • Coordinates committee work
  • Follows up on resolutions

Secretary

Runs administration.

  • Calls meetings, prepares agendas and minutes
  • Keeps the membership register and official records
  • Prepares annual reports; bank signatory

Vice Secretary

Supports the Secretary.

  • Acts as Secretary when needed
  • Supports records and communication between members

Treasurer

Keeps the finances.

  • Keeps financial records and reports quarterly and yearly
  • Banks all funds and tracks spending
  • Presents accounts to members; works with auditors; bank signatory

How members meet and decide

MeetingWhenQuorum
General AssemblyEvery April, August and DecemberHalf of registered members, plus one
Executive CommitteeAt least every two monthsTwo thirds of the committee
Annual General MeetingOnce a yearSet by the Constitution
Special General MeetingWhen something urgent comes upSet by the Constitution

Money

Every payment needs two authorised signatories, and one of them must be the Chairperson.
