Home / Programs

Fourteen program areas for refugees with disabilities

Each area comes from Article 7 of our Constitution. Together they cover rights, health, learning, income and community life.

All program areas

Rights and protection

Human rights and advocacy

Speaking up for the rights of refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities at community, county and national level.

Protection

Keeping refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities safe from harm, exploitation and abuse, with confidential ways to report.

Child protection

Protecting children with disabilities, and the children of parents with disabilities, from harm and neglect.

Gender equality and GBV prevention

Working for equal treatment of women and men, and to prevent gender-based violence in our communities.

Emergency response

Making sure people with disabilities are reached and not left behind when an emergency hits.

Health and wellbeing

Disability inclusion

Making services, humanitarian programs and community life accessible to people with disabilities.

Health

Helping persons with disabilities reach health care that works for them.

Mental health and psychosocial support

Support that helps people and families cope with displacement, loss and stress.

Learning and livelihoods

Education

Pushing for schools and training that children and adults with disabilities can attend.

Livelihoods

Vocational training, skills and income-generating activities, so members can earn a living.

Capacity building

Building the skills of our members and of other refugee-led organisations.

Research and documentation

Recording evidence and lived experience to shape policy, programs and advocacy.

Community and environment

Peacebuilding and social cohesion

Bringing refugee and host communities together to live peacefully side by side.

Climate action and resilience

Supporting member-led work on climate resilience and conservation.

Across every program

Alongside the fourteen areas, we run vocational and technical training, and use sports, arts and culture to build confidence and bring refugee and host communities together.

No organisation can do this alone. We work with government, UN agencies, refugee-led organisations, organisations of persons with disabilities, civil society, development partners, the private sector and universities.
