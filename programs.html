Our Programs & Areas of Operation

AMANI-RWDs designs and delivers programs across fourteen interconnected focus areas — each rooted in our mission to empower refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities through advocacy, inclusive development and sustainable partnerships.

♿

Disability Inclusion

Mainstreaming accessibility and disability-inclusive practice across humanitarian action, services and community life.

🛡

Protection

Safeguarding refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities from harm, exploitation and abuse, with confidential reporting mechanisms.

📚

Education

Advocating for inclusive education so children and adults with disabilities can learn without barriers.

❤

Health

Promoting equitable access to inclusive healthcare services for persons with disabilities.

💰

Livelihoods

Strengthening economic empowerment through vocational training, skills-building and income-generating opportunities.

🧠

Mental Health & Psychosocial Support (MHPSS)

Providing psychosocial support that helps individuals and families cope, heal and thrive.

👶

Child Protection

Protecting children with disabilities and children of persons with disabilities from harm and neglect.

♀

Gender Equality & GBV Prevention

Promoting gender equality and working to prevent Gender-Based Violence (GBV) within our communities.

🌍

Climate Action & Resilience

Supporting climate resilience and environmental conservation initiatives led by our members.

💬

Peacebuilding & Social Cohesion

Promoting peaceful coexistence and social cohesion between refugee and host communities.

⚖

Human Rights & Advocacy

Advocating for the rights of refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities at community, county and national levels.

🚨

Emergency Response

Responding to emergencies with a disability-inclusive lens, ensuring no one is left behind.

🏆

Capacity Building

Strengthening the skills and institutional capacity of refugee-led organizations and their members.

📋

Research & Documentation

Documenting evidence and lived experience to inform policy, programs and advocacy.

Cross-Cutting

Sports, Arts & Culture

Alongside our core focus areas, AMANI-RWDs facilitates vocational and technical training and promotes sports, arts and culture as tools for confidence, healing and community belonging.

How We Work

Built on Partnerships

No single organization can do this work alone. AMANI-RWDs collaborates with government institutions, refugee-led organizations, organizations of persons with disabilities, civil society organizations, UN agencies, development partners, the private sector and academic institutions.
