Partner with, volunteer for or give to AMANI-RWDs

AMANI is run by refugees with disabilities. Partners, volunteers and donors help that work reach further.

Partner with us

We work with organisations that want their programs to reach refugees with disabilities, and want those people involved in the design.

Who we work with

  • Government institutions
  • UN agencies
  • Refugee-led organisations
  • Organisations of persons with disabilities
  • Civil society organisations
  • Development partners
  • The private sector
  • Academic institutions

Start a partnership conversation

Volunteer

Volunteers support our work in disability inclusion, education, health outreach, psychosocial support, child protection, livelihoods training and community events.

Skills we can use: sign language and other interpretation, teaching, health care, logistics and advocacy. Volunteers can also join AMANI as members.

Give to our work
