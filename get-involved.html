Get Involved

There are many ways to stand with refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities — as a member, volunteer, donor or partner organization. This page is informational: there are no online accounts or logins on this site. To take the next step, simply reach out to us directly.

Membership

Who Can Join?

In line with Article 9 of our Constitution, membership is open to:

♿

Refugees & Asylum Seekers with Disabilities

The heart of our membership and our mission.

🤝

Parents & Caregivers

Family members who support and advocate alongside our members.

♥

Allies & Supporters

Persons without disabilities who support our objectives.

🎓

Professionals

Experts who contribute skills, knowledge and time.

✊

Volunteers

Hands-on contributors to programs and events.

🏢

Partner Organizations

Institutions collaborating on shared goals.

Membership Categories

  • Founder Members — the original members who established the Organization.
  • Ordinary Members — general members who meet the eligibility criteria.
  • Associate Members — supporting individuals or organizations.
  • Honorary Members — recognized for exceptional contribution.
Registration Fee. Membership is open upon payment of a non-refundable registration fee of Kshs 1,000 (Kenya Shillings One Thousand only), once eligibility conditions in the Constitution are met. The General Assembly sets registration and annual subscription fees. Contact us to begin the process — there is no online sign-up on this site.
Process

How to Join

  1. Reach out to us. Contact AMANI-RWDs to express your interest in membership, volunteering or partnership.
  2. Learn about eligibility. We'll walk you through the relevant category and any conditions set out in our Constitution.
  3. Complete registration. Eligible members pay the one-time, non-refundable registration fee (Kshs 1,000) and any applicable annual subscription.
  4. Participate. Attend the quarterly General Assembly meetings (April, August, December), vote on organizational matters, and help shape our work.
✊

Volunteer With Us

Volunteers support our programs across disability inclusion, education, health outreach, MHPSS, child protection, livelihoods training and community events. Whatever your skills — interpretation, teaching, healthcare, logistics, or advocacy — there is a way to contribute.

Give to Our Work

AMANI-RWDs is funded through membership fees, grants, donations, fundraising, income-generating activities, partnerships, consultancy and training services, and corporate sponsorships. Every shilling solely supports our mission — overseen by an authorized Treasurer and audited as required.

Official banking. AMANI-RWDs maintains its account with Equity Bank, Nakuru Branch (Gate House). For donation details and account information, please contact us directly — we do not publish account numbers on this website for security reasons.
