Why We Exist

A society where refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities enjoy equal rights, dignity and full participation in life.

Guided by the Constitution of Kenya (2010), the Refugees Act 2021, the Persons with Disabilities Act 2025, the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) and the 1951 Refugee Convention, AMANI-RWDs works at the intersection of displacement and disability — a space too often overlooked.

🎯

Our Vision

A society where refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities enjoy equal rights, opportunities, dignity and full participation in all aspects of life.

🤝

Our Mission

To empower refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities through advocacy, inclusive development, capacity building, protection, education, health, livelihoods and partnerships that promote independence and social inclusion.

✓

Our Legal Status

AMANI-RWDs is a non-profit, non-political, non-religious, refugee-led Community-Based Organization registered to promote dignity, inclusion, protection and socio-economic development.
