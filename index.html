Refugees with disabilities, running their own organisation in Nakuru.

AMANI-RWDs is a community-based organisation in Nakuru County, Kenya, led by refugees. We work for the rights, safety, health, learning and livelihoods of refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities.

A refugee-led organisation in Nakuru County

“We, the founder members of AMANI Refugees with Disabilities, recognize the dignity, equality and inherent rights of all refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities.”

From the preamble to our Constitution

Refugees with disabilities are easy to miss twice: by services built for refugees, and by services built for persons with disabilities. AMANI works where the two meet, and it is run by the people it serves.

Fourteen program areas, set in our Constitution

Article 7 of the AMANI Constitution sets out what we work toward. The areas fall into four groups.

Rights and protection

  • Human rights and advocacy
  • Protection
  • Child protection
  • Gender equality and GBV prevention
  • Emergency response

Health and wellbeing

  • Disability inclusion
  • Health
  • Mental health and psychosocial support

Learning and livelihoods

  • Education
  • Livelihoods
  • Capacity building
  • Research and documentation

Community and environment

  • Peacebuilding and social cohesion
  • Climate action and resilience

How AMANI is governed

Partners rightly ask how a community organisation is run. These are the rules in our Constitution.

See our leadership and founders

  • Legal statusNon-profit, non-political, non-religious community-based organisation
  • Registered officeNakuru County, Kenya
  • Members decideThe General Assembly meets every April, August and December
  • Elected leadersFive office bearers, elected by the members for three-year terms
  • MoneyTwo authorised signatories approve every payment, one of them the Chairperson. Banked with Equity Bank, Nakuru
  • SafeguardingZero tolerance for sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment, with confidential reporting
