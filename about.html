About AMANI Refugees with Disabilities

A non-profit, non-political, non-religious, refugee-led community-based organisation, registered in Nakuru County, Kenya.

Vision and mission

Our vision

A society where refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities enjoy equal rights, opportunities, dignity and full participation in all aspects of life.

Our mission

To empower refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities through advocacy, inclusive development, capacity building, protection, education, health, livelihoods and partnerships that promote independence and social inclusion.

Our name and our emblem

Amani is Swahili for peace. Our emblem shows a dove and a wheelchair user inside a globe, five people of different colours reaching upward, held by two hands under a rising sun.

Our motto says what we are for: Empowering Abilities, Inspiring Inclusion, Transforming Lives.

Our core values

Every program, partnership and decision is held to these.

  • Human dignity
  • Inclusion
  • Equality
  • Respect for diversity
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Integrity
  • Participation
  • Gender equality
  • Innovation
  • Teamwork
  • Respect for human rights

The laws we work under

Our Constitution is the supreme governing document of AMANI. It is guided by these.

  • The Constitution of Kenya, 2010
  • The Refugees Act, 2021
  • The Persons with Disabilities Act, 2025
  • The UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities
  • The 1951 Refugee Convention

Non-discrimination and accessibility

No one is turned away because of disability, nationality, refugee status, age, gender, ethnicity, religion or political opinion.

We provide reasonable accommodation, including sign language interpretation, Braille, large print and physically accessible venues, wherever we reasonably can. This website is built to the same standard: it works with screen readers and keyboards, and its type is designed for readers with low vision.

Safeguarding

Zero tolerance for sexual exploitation and abuse, sexual harassment, child abuse, abuse of vulnerable adults, exploitation and discrimination, with confidential reporting. How to raise a concern
