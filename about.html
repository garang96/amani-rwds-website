About AMANI-RWDs

We, the founder members of AMANI Refugees with Disabilities (AMANI-RWDs), recognize the dignity, equality and inherent rights of all refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities. This Constitution is the supreme governing instrument of our Organization.

🎯

Our Vision

A society where refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities enjoy equal rights, opportunities, dignity and full participation in all aspects of life.

🤝

Our Mission

To empower refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities through advocacy, inclusive development, capacity building, protection, education, health, livelihoods and partnerships that promote independence and social inclusion.

Motto

Empowering Abilities, Inspiring Inclusion, Transforming Lives.

Legal Standing

A Refugee-Led Community-Based Organization

AMANI-RWDs is a non-profit, non-political, non-religious, refugee-led Community-Based Organization established to promote the dignity, inclusion, protection, empowerment and socio-economic development of refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities.

🏠

Registered Office

Nakuru County, Kenya. Branch offices may be established anywhere in Kenya upon approval of the General Assembly.

⚖

Guiding Legal Framework

The Constitution of Kenya (2010), the Refugees Act 2021, the Persons with Disabilities Act 2025, the UN CRPD, the 1951 Refugee Convention and related human rights instruments.

🤝

Founding Principle

Founded on inclusion, accountability, participation, transparency, equality, non-discrimination, respect for diversity and sustainable development.

Our Commitments

Objectives

Article 7 of our Constitution sets out what we work toward every day.

  • Promote rights of refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities.
  • Advocate for inclusive education at every level.
  • Promote inclusive healthcare access and services.
  • Strengthen livelihoods and economic empowerment.
  • Promote mental health & psychosocial support (MHPSS).
  • Prevent Gender-Based Violence (GBV).
  • Promote child protection across all programs.
  • Advocate for disability-inclusive humanitarian action.
  • Conduct awareness campaigns against discrimination.
  • Strengthen refugee-led organizations.
  • Promote peacebuilding and social cohesion.
  • Facilitate vocational and technical training.
  • Promote sports, arts and culture.
  • Support climate resilience and build partnerships with governments, UN agencies, NGOs, donors and the private sector.
Accessibility

Non-Discrimination & Accessibility

No person shall be discriminated against on the basis of disability, nationality, refugee status, age, gender, ethnicity, religion or political opinion. We promote accessibility through reasonable accommodation, including sign language interpretation, Braille, large print and physically accessible venues wherever reasonably practicable.

Safeguarding. AMANI-RWDs maintains zero tolerance for sexual exploitation and abuse, sexual harassment, child abuse, abuse of vulnerable adults, exploitation and discrimination, backed by confidential reporting and safeguarding mechanisms.
