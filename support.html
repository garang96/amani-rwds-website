Support for refugees with disabilities in Nakuru

For refugees and asylum seekers with disabilities living in Nakuru County, and for the parents and caregivers who support them.

What AMANI can help with

Your rights

Understanding your rights as a refugee or asylum seeker with a disability in Kenya, and help to claim them.

Staying safe

Protection from harm, exploitation and abuse. You can report a concern in confidence.

Health care

Support to reach health services that work for persons with disabilities.

School and training

Pushing for inclusive education, and vocational and technical training for adults.

Mental health

Psychosocial support for you and your family.

Earning a living

Skills, training and income-generating activities.

Children

Protection for children with disabilities, and for the children of parents with disabilities.

Sports, arts and culture

Activities that build confidence and bring people together.

How to reach us

You do not need any papers to ask for help. You can bring a family member, a caregiver or a friend.

  1. Get in touch. Contact AMANI, or ask a community contact point or a trusted referral partner in Nakuru County to connect you with us.
  2. Tell us what you need. We listen first, in the language you are most comfortable with where we can.
  3. Agree the next step together. That may be support from one of our programs, a referral, or joining AMANI as a member.

Tell us about your access needs

Our Constitution commits us to sign language interpretation, Braille, large print and accessible venues wherever we reasonably can. Let us know what you need to take part.

Membership

Members run AMANI. They meet at the General Assembly in April, August and December, vote on decisions and elect the Executive Committee.

  • Who can joinRefugees and asylum seekers with disabilities, their parents and caregivers, allies, professionals, volunteers and partner organisations
  • CategoriesFounder, Ordinary, Associate and Honorary members
  • Registration feeKshs 1,000, one time and non-refundable. The General Assembly sets any annual subscription
  • How to joinContact us. We explain which category fits you and what the Constitution asks of members

Raising a concern

AMANI has zero tolerance for sexual exploitation and abuse, sexual harassment, child abuse, abuse of vulnerable adults, exploitation and discrimination.

If something has happened to you or someone you know, contact us and say it is a safeguarding concern. It will be handled in confidence.
